Try it

  • Click a cell to edit it, or select one and type. Formulas start with =: =SUM(C2:C13), =IF(E2, C2, 0).
  • Drag or Shift+arrows to select a range, then Ctrl+D to fill down; references shift.
  • Copy a range and paste it into another spreadsheet app, or paste one in.
  • Make a cycle (=C2 in C2) and see #CYCLE! instead of a hang.
  • Edits from other windows, or Pericles, flash with their author's name.
