Ἡράκλειτος

On nature, in pieces

Select any words to mark or copy them. Dotted words have glosses.

  1. Listening not to me but to the logos, it is wise to agree that all things are one.
  2. This kosmos, the same for all, no god made and no man; it always was, and is, and will be an ever-living fire, kindled in measures and quenched in measures.
  3. War is the father of all and the king of all. Some it shows as gods and some as men; some it makes slaves and some free.
  4. You cannot step twice into the same river: other and still other waters flow on.
  5. Aion is a child at play, moving pieces on a board. The kingship belongs to a child.
  6. You would not find the limits of the psyche though you walked every road, so deep is its logos.
  7. The road up and the road down are one and the same.
  8. Nature loves to hide.
  9. Dogs bark at anyone they don’t know.
  10. A person’s character is their daimon.
  11. Corpses should be thrown out sooner than dung.
  12. Most people do not understand the things they come across, nor do they grasp them when taught, though they think they do.
